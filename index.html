How I work

Three moves, repeated until the work is real.

The order changes. The mix changes. The pattern does not.

01
Step 01

Get to the first real version fast.

The first useful version does not need to look finished. It needs to prove that the thing can exist.

  • Strip the idea down to the part that matters.
  • Build something testable as early as possible.
  • Use the prototype to force better decisions.
Step 02

Keep the engineering honest.

Robotics and electronics do not care about how good the pitch sounds. They either work or they do not.

  • Stay close to the technical detail.
  • Solve the real constraint instead of narrating around it.
  • Make sure the working version is not pretending.
Step 03

Give the work a company and a clear message.

When the product starts to work, the next job is making sure other people understand why it matters.

  • Build the company around the work.
  • Explain it clearly enough that other people get it fast.
  • Let results create trust, press, and interest.
