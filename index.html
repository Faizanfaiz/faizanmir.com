About

From building robots in Kashmir to AI-powered mobility in the UK.

I'm an entrepreneur and engineer driven by one idea: technology should solve real problems — whether that's helping someone regain mobility, making transport safer, or letting the next generation of engineers learn through robotics.

My work spans robotics, AI, and hardware engineering. I've founded and co-founded ventures across micromobility safety, assistive exoskeletons, educational robotics, and mission-critical remote control systems — and I like the messy early phase where something has to actually function before anyone gets excited about it.

Ventures

Companies I've built and led.

Four ventures across mobility safety, assistive tech, educational robotics, and remote infrastructure.

Founder & CEO 2024 — Now

Nearhuman

Redefining micromobility safety with computer-vision AI safety kits for e-scooters and electric mobility vehicles. Patent filed, prototype shipped for operator testing, and pilots progressing with leading operators and OEMs from the Bristol Robotics Lab incubator. Finalist for Innovative StartUp of the Year at the UK StartUp Awards 2026.

Visit Nearhuman →
Co-Founder 2021 — 2022

Tribonix

Electronics design and motion control. Worked extensively with the CAN protocol for brushless actuators and CAD modelling that contributed to an assistive exoskeleton for people with muscular dystrophy — tech-driven social impact.

Electronics & assistive robotics
Co-Founder 2021 — 2022

Sefarium

A Kashmir-based robotics startup that began with delivery robots and pivoted toward educational and industrial robotics — building robotic learning platforms now used by universities and schools.

Educational & industrial robotics
Co-Founder & CTO 2018 — 2020

Airwai

Cloud-based remote control systems for telecom sites near the Line of Control, where snowfall makes access impossible. Internet-connected hardware kept gensets — and connectivity — running in extreme conditions. The systems remain operational across Kashmir today.

Mission-critical remote infrastructure
